# Tom W Tyler - Chimney Sweeping Booking Policy

## 1. How to Book & Appointment Scheduling

You can book a chimney sweep with me through any of the following methods:

- **Online:** Submit a booking request directly via my website at [twtsweep.co.uk](https://www.twtsweep.co.uk/#bookingpolicy).
- **Phone / Text / WhatsApp:** Contact me directly to arrange a suitable date and time.
- **Email:** Send your enquiry including your postcode and appliance details, and I will confirm available time slots.

**Appliance and fuel type:** When booking, please tell me what type of appliance you have (for example, open fire, wood burner or multi-fuel stove) and what fuel you burn.

**Landlords and letting agents:** When booking, please tell me who will be paying for the work, and where the invoice and the certificate should be sent. If a tenant will be giving me access, please make sure they know about the appointment and the preparation requirements in section 2.

**Arrival times:** I aim to arrive promptly within your agreed arrival window. However, due to traffic, weather, or unexpected complications on earlier jobs, arrival times are estimates. I will keep you informed if schedules shift significantly.

**Your information:** Details you give me when booking are handled in line with my Privacy Policy.

## 2. Appliance Preparation, Home Care & Safety

- **Cooling Period & Fuel Clearing:** For safety and to prevent damage to equipment, your appliance or open fire must **not** have been used for **at least 12 hours** before I arrive. Please make sure all ash and unspent fuel are completely cleared from the grate. If the appliance is still warm or hot, I will be unable to sweep.
- **Property Protection & Clear Access:** I take reasonable steps to protect your property, using protective sheets and equipment to minimise dust. Please make sure the hearth and surrounding area are completely clear of furniture, ornaments, and fragile items so I can set up safely.
- **Child and Pet Safety:** For their own protection, and to prevent accidental injury or interference with my equipment, please make sure children and pets are safely supervised in a separate room for the duration of my visit.

## 3. Access, Height & Roof Work Limitations

- **No Roof or Ladder Work:** I do not undertake any roof or ladder work. All sweeping and visual inspections are carried out internally from the hearth or appliance access points. If an issue requires external roof access, or work on cowls or chimney pots, I will advise you to engage a suitable specialist. I am not responsible for the work or services of any third party.
- **Unsafe or Inaccessible Flues:** If I cannot safely access your chimney, or if the installation poses an immediate health and safety risk, I reserve the right to abort the job.

## 4. Cancellations, Rescheduling & Unserviceable Visits

- **If You Need to Cancel:** Please give me at least **48 hours' notice** if you need to cancel or change your appointment. There is no charge for cancelling with 48 hours' notice or more. If you cancel with less notice, or are not available when I arrive, I reserve the right to charge an abortive visit fee of **up to the full cost of the appointment**.
- **If I Need to Reschedule:** On rare occasions, due to illness, vehicle breakdown, extreme weather, or an emergency on an earlier job, I may need to reschedule your appointment. I will always give you as much notice as possible and work with you to find the next available time slot.
- **Unserviceable Visits:** If I arrive for a scheduled appointment and am unable to carry out the work because of an uncooled appliance, an uncleared grate, an unsafe setup, or a lack of access to the property, I reserve the right to charge an abortive visit fee of up to the full cost of the appointment.

## 5. Heavy Deposits, Obstructions & Return Visits

- **Heavy Deposits:** Some chimneys have heavy deposits, such as tar or creosote glaze, that cannot be fully dealt with in a single standard visit. This may need further treatment or a return visit.
- **Nests and Obstructions:** If I find a nest or other obstruction, I may need to return to complete the work, either because further treatment is needed or because there isn't enough time to finish safely.
- **Further Charges:** In both cases, further charges will apply. I will explain what is needed and the likely cost before going ahead.

## 6. Pre-Existing Damage & Limitations of Inspection

- **Structural Limitations:** Chimneys and flues degrade over time through age, weathering, and operational heat. I am not liable for pre-existing structural defects, loose brickwork, crumbling mortar, or failing flue liners discovered during sweeping.
- **Not a Full Structural Survey:** The visual check I carry out during a sweep is a standard operational safety check, **not a full structural survey**. It cannot guarantee that the flue or appliance is free from hidden structural flaws, internal cracks, or concealed defects.
- **My Responsibility:** Nothing in this policy limits my liability for death or personal injury caused by my negligence, for damage caused by my failure to use reasonable care and skill in carrying out the work, or for any other liability that cannot be excluded by law.

## 7. Insurance & Credentials

- **Public Liability Insurance:** I hold Public Liability Insurance covering work undertaken at your property.
- **Waste Carrier Registration:** I am a registered lower-tier waste carrier, so soot and debris removed from your chimney are handled and disposed of in line with environmental regulations.

## 8. Payment Terms

Payment is due on completion of the service or within **7 days** of the invoice being issued.

- **Accepted Payment Methods:** Bank Transfer (BACS), Cash, or Cheque.
- **Please Note:** I do not accept credit or debit cards.

## 9. Certification, Warnings & Complaints Procedure

- **Certificates & Warnings:** On successful completion of the sweep, I will issue an official NACS Industry Certificate of Chimney Sweeping for your household records and insurance purposes. If an appliance or flue is deemed unsafe to use, I will issue a formal Warning Notice setting out the risks identified.
- **Complaints Procedure:** If you are dissatisfied with any aspect of my service, please contact me directly using the details on my website, as soon as possible and ideally within 7 days of the visit, so I can investigate and resolve the issue promptly. If we are unable to resolve a dispute about my sweeping standard directly, you may escalate the matter to the NACS Head Office for formal trade association guidance.
