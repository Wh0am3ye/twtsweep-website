1. Full About Me Page
About Me
Hi, I’m Tom, the person behind TWTSweep, a NACS-registered chimney sweep covering Gwynedd, Conwy and Anglesey.
The name felt right from the start. Twt means ‘tidy’ in Welsh, which seemed like a reasonable thing to promise as a chimney sweep. It also happens to be my initials. Small detail, but it felt like a good way to root the business in the place I’ve chosen to call home.
How I ended up here
Before training as a chimney sweep, I spent many years working with young people, first as a youth worker, and most recently in a special needs school supporting teenagers with additional learning needs. It was meaningful work, and it taught me a lot about patience, reliability and earning trust. After taking time out to look after my children, I needed work that would fit around family life. TWTSweep is what came of that, and it turns out I really enjoy it.
I launched TWTSweep in March 2026, and I’ve been building the business the way I think a good local trade should be, quietly, carefully, one job at a time.
The work itself
I offer chimney sweeping for open fires, wood-burning stoves and range cookers, as well as CCTV flue inspections and blockage and nest removal. After every sweep, I issue a NACS Certificate of Sweeping, useful for insurance purposes and worth keeping for your records.
On the day, I’ll arrive prepared, work cleanly using dust sheets and protective equipment, and leave your home exactly as I found it. I’ll communicate clearly before and after the job, so you always know what to expect and what I’ve found.
Serving North Wales
I cover most of North Wales, Gwynedd, Conwy and Anglesey. From Abergele to Aberdaron, Benllech to Betws-y-Coed and Holyhead to Harlech, and everywhere in between. If you’re not sure whether I cover your location, just get in touch and I’ll let you know.
A note on Welsh
Dw i’n dysgu Cymraeg, and while I’m not fluent yet, it matters to me to make the effort. My website and some communications are available in both Welsh and English, and that’s something I’ll keep building on.
Not sure if you need a sweep?
If you’re unsure whether your chimney or stove needs attention, please don’t hesitate to get in touch. I’d rather give you an honest answer, even if that answer is “it’s fine for now”, than have you worry unnecessarily. You can book online at a time that suits you, or simply drop me a message if you’d prefer a conversation first.
